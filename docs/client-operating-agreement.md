# Client Operating Agreement

**Reusable addendum / kickoff terms for independent consulting engagements.**  
Paste into an SOW, Exhibit A, or send as a Slack/email kickoff. Fill the bracketed fields per client.

---

## How I work

I provide services as an **independent consultant** on a **self-managed schedule**. I do not keep fixed daily shift hours. Clients engage me for **outcomes**, not presence on a clock.

### What you get

1. **Deliverables** — agreed work products and results, on the timelines we set together  
2. **Meetings** — I attend booked calls and sessions on time  
3. **Response standard** — on active threads and assigned work, I respond within **[X hours / same business day]** unless we agree otherwise for a specific launch or incident  
4. **Pro / stakeholder timing** — where the work depends on external parties (e.g. customers, pros), I manage my own day to match when those conversations actually move  

### What I don’t sell

- Always-on availability or sub-minute reply expectations  
- Fixed daily blocks unless we explicitly agree them in writing for a short window  
- Exclusive dedication of my calendar (engagements are **non-exclusive** unless a signed agreement says otherwise)  

Offline time — rest, health, family, and other non-competing client work — is normal and expected. That is how I stay effective.

### How we handle asks

- **In-scope asks during active work:** I confirm scope and ETA.  
- **Asks that land when I’m offline:** I pick them up when back online and give a realistic ETA. An acknowledgment without an ETA is not a commitment to finish before I’m next online.  
- **Urgent:** Say so. I’ll tell you what I can do and by when.  

### Competitive / conflicts

I won’t take work that conflicts with my ability to deliver for you, or that competes with your business where a signed agreement restricts it. Other **non-competing** clients are allowed.

### Hours and fees

- Fees and any hour cap: **as stated in the signed Project Assignment / SOW**  
- No implied employee schedule, bonus, or benefits unless written  

### Changes

Any change to response standards, exclusivity, or required hours must be **mutual and in writing** (email or signed amendment is enough).

> I work as an independent consultant on a self-managed schedule — no fixed daily hours. I’ll hit agreed deliverables, attend booked meetings, and keep a clear response standard on active work (typically within **[X hours]**). Outside of that I’m offline by design; that’s how I stay effective across clients. Late asks get a realistic ETA when I’m back online — an ack without an ETA isn’t a same-block commitment. Urgent? Say so and I’ll tell you what I can do and by when.
